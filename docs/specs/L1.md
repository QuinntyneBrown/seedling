# L1 - High-Level Requirements

> Each L1 names a capability the Seedlings system shall deliver. Detailed, testable behaviours live in
> `L2.md`. Number in order; never renumber. Revise in place and date the revision.
>
> Revised 2026-10-29: the previous requirements set was replaced by the Seedlings RESP requirements.

Seedlings is an installable, responsive progressive web app (PWA) that acts as the interface to an
agent which opens a Registered Education Savings Plan (RESP) for a child and claims the Canada
Learning Bond (CLB) and Canada Education Savings Grant (CESG) on the family's behalf, then keeps the
account on track over time.

## L1-001: Installable Progressive Web App
Seedlings shall be a progressive web app with a manifest and service worker, installable to the
home screen on iOS, Android, Windows and macOS.

## L1-002: Responsive Layout
Seedlings shall render a complete, usable experience on every screen size from 320 px phones
through tablets to 1920 px and wider desktops, in portrait and landscape, with no horizontal
scrolling and no loss of function.

## L1-003: Offline Tolerance
Seedlings shall load its shell offline, show the last known case status, and queue uploads and form
inputs until connectivity returns.

## L1-004: Performance
Seedlings shall be interactive within 4 seconds on a mid-range Android phone over 4G, and its API
shall answer parent-facing requests fast enough that no screen waits noticeably on the server.

## L1-005: Parent Account and Child Profiles
A parent shall be able to create a verified account with email or phone, sign in on any device, and
add one or more children, each with its own RESP case.

## L1-006: Consent
Seedlings shall obtain explicit parent consent, with a plain-language reason, before collecting any
identity document, Social Insurance Number (SIN) or financial information.

## L1-007: Eligibility Check First
From a short guided intake, Seedlings shall tell the parent whether each child qualifies for the
Canada Learning Bond and the CESG, and the dollar amount, before asking for any documents.

## L1-008: Resumable Intake
Seedlings shall collect what is needed to open an RESP and claim the bond through a step-by-step
intake that saves progress automatically and resumes on any device.

## L1-009: Document Capture
Seedlings shall let the parent photograph or upload required documents with the device camera,
assess image quality, and request a retake when needed.

## L1-010: Agent-Driven Case
Each child's RESP shall be a case worked by an agent that selects a no-fee provider accepting
zero-contribution CLB accounts, pre-fills the provider application and the government grant forms,
submits the claims, and tracks them to completion.

## L1-011: Parent Approval Gates
Seedlings shall require explicit parent approval before any application is submitted, any account is
opened, or any information is shared with a third party.

## L1-012: Status in Plain Language
Seedlings shall always show the current status of each case, what is happening, and what, if
anything, the parent needs to do.

## L1-013: Human Handoff
When the agent cannot complete a step, Seedlings shall route the case to a human reviewer and tell
the parent.

## L1-014: Grant Tracking and Nudges
After the RESP is open, Seedlings shall confirm when CLB and CESG deposits land, alert the parent if
one is missing, and remind them of remaining CESG room each year without pressuring families who
cannot contribute.

## L1-015: Life Changes
Seedlings shall let the parent report changes such as a new address, new child, custody change or
income change, and update the case and affected claims.

## L1-016: Notifications
Seedlings shall notify the parent of status changes, approvals needed and deposits received by push,
SMS or email, as the parent chooses.

## L1-017: Language Support
Seedlings shall support English and French, with all parent-facing text at a Grade 6 reading level.

## L1-018: Pricing and Payments
Seedlings shall support a one-time launch fee, an optional annual care plan, and a free tier funded
by provider referral fees, with card payments through a hosted provider that never exposes card
numbers to Seedlings.

## L1-019: Data Protection
All personal and financial data shall be encrypted in transit and at rest; SINs and identity
documents shall be held in a segregated, access-logged store, shown only after re-authentication,
and deleted once the provider confirms the account is open unless the parent chooses to keep them.
Every request shall be authenticated and authorized, every external input validated, and no secret
or personal information written to logs in plaintext.

## L1-020: Privacy and Audit
Seedlings shall comply with PIPEDA, let the parent view, export and delete their data, and log every
agent action, parent approval and third-party submission with a timestamp the parent can see for
their own case.

## L1-021: Guided Onboarding Wizard with Visible Roadmap
After registration, the parent shall be taken through a single guided wizard, one focused question
per screen, that ends with the account, children, language, contact preference and eligibility all
set up and the first claim ready to start. Before the first step the wizard shall state the total
expected time. Throughout, it shall show a roadmap of all steps with the current step, steps done,
time spent and time remaining, so a parent can stop at any point and resume later exactly where they
left off. The wizard is the primary path into the app and shall be usable by a parent with no
technical background.

## L1-022: Accessibility
Every parent-facing screen shall meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA and be fully usable with a keyboard, a
screen reader and touch alone.
