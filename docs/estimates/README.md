## Roadmap

1. identity-and-access and privacy-and-security: sign-up, sessions, case authorization, vault, validation, redaction, encryption, audit.
2. web-experience: accessible, responsive, installable, offline-capable shell with language and performance budgets.
3. onboarding and eligibility: the wizard, its roadmap, stop and resume, eligibility check and add child.
4. intake and billing: consent, intake steps, document capture, plan selection and payment.
5. case-agent then account-care: provider, pre-fill, approval, tracking, review handoff, then deposits, reminders, channels and life changes.
