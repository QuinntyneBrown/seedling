Why we need each detail

Read what we collect and who sees it. One agreement covers it all, and you can take it back in Settings.

  • Your SIN and each child’s SINThe government pays the Canada Learning Bond only into an RESP linked to the child’s SIN. Shared with your RESP provider and Employment and Social Development Canada.
  • Legal names and dates of birthTo match you and your children to government records. Shared with the same two.
  • Your home addressThe RESP provider must confirm where you live. Shared with your RESP provider.
  • Photos of your ID and a recent billProof of identity and address for the provider. Deleted 30 days after your claim is sent.
  • Any existing RESPSo we don’t open a second one by mistake. Stays with us.

We never sell your data or use it for ads. Read the privacy notice
