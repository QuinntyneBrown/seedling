Step 7 of 8

The next steps need some private details

To open an RESP and claim the grants, the bank and the government need to know exactly who you and your children are. Here’s what we’ll ask for, and why.

  • Social Insurance Numbers

    Yours and each child’s. The government pays grants only to an RESP linked to the child’s SIN.

  • Legal names, birth dates and your address

    So the RESP provider can confirm who you are.

  • Photos of two documents

    Your ID and a proof of address. Deleted 30 days after your claim is sent, unless you choose otherwise.

Everything is encrypted. We ask you to confirm it’s you before showing a saved SIN or document.
